\begin{corollary}[Finite-window rate form]
Let $m\geq0$ be an integer and let $(A_k)_{k\geq1}$ be events on a
probability space. Say that the sequence is $m$-dependent if, whenever
$I,J\subseteq\mathbb{N}_{>0}$ are finite and $|i-j|>m$ for every $i\in I$ and
$j\in J$, the sigma-algebras generated by $(A_i)_{i\in I}$ and
$(A_j)_{j\in J}$ are independent. Let
$\phi:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{N}$ be non-decreasing and suppose
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{\phi(n)}\mathbb{P}(A_k)\geq n
\qquad\text{for every }n\geq1.
\]
Then, for every $N\geq1$ and every $i\geq0$,
\[
\mathbb{P}\!\left(\bigcup_{k=i+1}^{\phi(i+N)}A_k\right)
\geq1-\exp\!\left(-\frac{N}{m+1}\right).
\]
\end{corollary}
