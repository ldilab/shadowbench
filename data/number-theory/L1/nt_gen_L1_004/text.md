\begin{lemma}
Define the ternary quadratic form
\[
Q_3(x,y,z)=x^2+3y^2+2yz+4z^2.
\]
Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer whose residue modulo $11$ is a quadratic nonresidue. Then $11n$ is not represented by $Q_3$ over the integers; that is, there are no $x,y,z\in\mathbb{Z}$ such that
\[
Q_3(x,y,z)=11n.
\]
\end{lemma}
