\begin{lemma}
Define the harmonic numbers and the second-order harmonic numbers by
\[
H_n=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{j},
\qquad
H_{n,2}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{1}{j^2}.
\]
The following identities hold.

1. For all positive integers $k$ and $m$,
\[
\sum_{r=1}^{m}\binom{r-1}{k-1}=\binom{m}{k}.
\]

2. For every positive integer $n$,
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n}\binom{n}{k}\frac{(-1)^{k-1}}{k}H_k=H_{n,2}.
\]
\end{lemma}
