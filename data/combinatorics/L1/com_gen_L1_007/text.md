A finite family \(\mathcal B\) of sets is a \emph{sunflower with core \(C\)} if
\[
U\cap U'=C
\]
for every two distinct members \(U,U'\in\mathcal B\). A \emph{\(k\)-sunflower} is a
sunflower consisting of exactly \(k\) sets.

\begin{lemma}[Erdős–Rado Sunflower Lemma]
Let \(s\ge 1\) and \(k\ge 2\) be integers. If \(\mathcal F\) is a finite family of sets, each
of cardinality at most \(s\), and
\[
|\mathcal F|>(k-1)^s s!,
\]
then \(\mathcal F\) contains a \(k\)-sunflower.
\end{lemma}
